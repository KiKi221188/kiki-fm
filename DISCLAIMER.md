# Disclaimer

KiKi FM is an independent radio directory and player. It does not host, produce, record, or own any of the audio content listed on the site.

## How playback works

Every station's audio stream is served directly from that station's own broadcast servers. When a visitor presses Play, their browser connects straight to the station's stream URL — KiKi FM does not proxy, cache, re-stream, or store any audio.

## Responsibility for content

Individual radio stations and their stream providers are solely responsible for the content they broadcast and for holding any necessary rights and licences for that content. KiKi FM's own material — its code, design, and written descriptions — is original; station names, logos, and descriptions used in listings should only be ones the site operator has permission to use, and should not be copied verbatim from any third-party directory or station's own materials.

## Sample data included in this repository

The stations shipped in `src/data/stations.json` at the time this project was generated are **fictional placeholder entries** for demonstration purposes, pointed at freely available test audio streams so the player works out of the box. They are not real Tamil radio stations. Before going live, replace every entry with real stations you have confirmed you're permitted to list, and real stream URLs supplied or approved by those stations.
